ADR-005: Authentication Strategy
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-01-01

Context:
Buckeye Marketplace is targeted at Ohio State students. Users need a trusted, low-friction way to sign up and log in. Fake or unverified accounts are a marketplace safety risk.

Decision:
Use OAuth (Google / OSU login) for authentication.

Reasons:
* No new passwords for users to manage
* Fast signup and onboarding
* OAuth via institutional login (OSU) provides trust and verification
* Reduces fake accounts
* Improves recruiter trust in the platform

Consequences:
* Users must have a Google or OSU account (acceptable for OSU-targeted platform)
* No custom password reset flow needed
* Speeds up onboarding significantly for the target audience
